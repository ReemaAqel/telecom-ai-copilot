# Demo Telecom Company Technical Support Policy

This is a fictional policy for educational purposes and does not represent any real company.

## Troubleshooting Slow Internet

1. Check the customer's used and remaining data allowance.
2. If the data allowance is exhausted, review the plan's speed reduction policy.
3. If the data allowance is not exhausted, ask the customer to restart their device.
4. Ask the customer to check the network signal strength and try connecting from another location.
5. If the issue persists, a technical support ticket can be created.

## Creating a Support Ticket

A ticket requires the customer ID, an issue title, and an issue description.
A ticket summary must be shown to the customer, and their confirmation must be obtained before creating the ticket.
The initial ticket status is Open.
After the ticket is successfully saved, its ID must be shown to the customer.
Do not claim that a ticket has been created unless it has been successfully saved.

## Issue Resolution Time

This policy does not specify a fixed issue resolution time.
Do not promise a resolution deadline without reliable information.

## Customer Data Protection

Customers may only access their own account data.
Do not disclose another customer's data.
Do not ask customers to provide their passwords or verification codes.

## Unavailable Information

If information is not available in the documents or accessible tools,
clearly state that there is insufficient information instead of guessing the answer.
