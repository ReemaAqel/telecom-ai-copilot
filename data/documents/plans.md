# Demo Telecom Company Plans

This is fictional data for educational purposes and does not represent any real company's offers.

## Start Plan
Plan ID: START
Monthly price: 10 Jordanian dinars (JOD).
Monthly data allowance: 20 GB.
After the data allowance is exhausted, the speed is reduced to 1 Mbps.

## Plus Plan
Plan ID: PLUS
Monthly price: 15 Jordanian dinars (JOD).
Monthly data allowance: 50 GB.
After the data allowance is exhausted, the speed is reduced to 2 Mbps.

## Max Plan
Plan ID: MAX
Monthly price: 25 Jordanian dinars (JOD).
Monthly data allowance: 100 GB.
After the data allowance is exhausted, the speed is reduced to 5 Mbps.

## Data Renewal
The data allowance resets at the beginning of the next billing cycle.
Unused data does not roll over to the following month.

## Plan Changes
Plan changes take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle.
